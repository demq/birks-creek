Community meeting with Unley Council

A chance for Birks and Myra Street residents to show Council the source, extent and ongoing impact of the groundwater released down our street, and to work towards a practical, long-term solution. The Council Assets Manager and both Parkside Ward Councillors will attend.

When
Monday 9 November 2026
5:00–6:00pm
Where
Outside 4 Birks St, Parkside
(just up from the shores of Lake Myra, you can't miss it)
